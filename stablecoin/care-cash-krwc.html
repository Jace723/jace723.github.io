What is Care Cash (KRWC)?

Definition · Care Cash (KRWC)Care Cash (KRWC) is a proposed won-referenced payment instrument for conditional care settlement, designed by Jace Shim (2026) and developed from Chapter 15 of Target Money. The design white paper describes a settlement system and its prerequisites: issuance and redemption, reserves, conditional release of care payments, and implementation gates.

Design statusThe design remains conceptual and experimental. It provides no evidence of authorization, live issuance, or operational performance.

The proposed interface combines a wallet and merchant interface, a sponsored-fee option, conditional settlement, and external records. Track who actually pays sponsored fees and account for this cost independently of the settlement amount.

Reserve and redemption

The proposed design targets par redemption and tests a reserve mix of 30% won deposits and 70% short-dated Korean sovereign or monetary-stabilization instruments.

Care settlement and safeguards

1

Commit

A payer commits funds for defined care units.

2

Claim

A provider supplies a claim against those units.

3

Record

The evidence keeper records documentation in a restricted system and communicates an approval or exception reference.

4

Release

Supported amounts may be released while disputed amounts await review.

The parties agree on notification, evidence correction, appeal, and refund procedures. Minimize personal data disclosure and preserve alternatives for users who lack smartphone access.

Implementation gates

GateBefore moving on
1 · Legal authorityEstablish who has legal authority to issue, hold reserves, and handle redemption, before real-value activity
2 · Legal reviewReview privacy, consumer remedies, financial-crime controls, insolvency treatment, and permitted responses to lawful orders with jurisdiction-specific counsel
3 · Synthetic testingTest reconciliations, authorizations, controlled upgrades, and incident recovery with synthetic funds
4 · Assurance and experienceEvaluate external assurance, governance separation, and participant experience
5 · Limited pilotOnly then consider a limited pilot

Frequently asked questions

What is Care Cash (KRWC)?

Care Cash (KRWC) is a proposed won-referenced payment instrument for conditional care settlement, designed by Jace Shim (2026) and developed from Chapter 15 of Target Money. The design white paper describes a settlement system and its prerequisites: issuance and redemption, reserves, conditional release of care payments, and implementation gates.

Is KRWC a live or authorized stablecoin?

No. The white paper describes a conceptual and experimental design. It provides no evidence of authorization, live issuance, or operational performance, and records no approval or legal finding.

What reserves does the KRWC design propose?

The design targets par redemption and tests an illustrative reserve mix of 30% won deposits and 70% short-dated Korean sovereign or monetary-stabilization instruments, with daily reconciliation of token supply against custodian and bank records and redemption stress tests.

How would KRWC settle care payments?

A payer commits funds for defined care units, a provider claims against those units, an evidence keeper records documentation in a restricted system and communicates an approval or exception reference, and supported amounts may be released while disputed amounts await review.

What must happen before KRWC could be piloted?

Establish legal authority to issue, hold reserves, and handle redemption; review privacy, consumer remedies, financial-crime controls, insolvency, and lawful-order responses with jurisdiction-specific counsel; test with synthetic funds; and evaluate external assurance, governance separation, and participant experience before considering a limited pilot.
