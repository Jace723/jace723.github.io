What is the Exogenous Value Monetization Model?

Definition · Exogenous Value Monetization ModelThe Exogenous Value Monetization Model (외생 가치 화폐화 모델), proposed by Jace Shim in Target Money (2026), is the fourth vector of his Four Vectors of Trust Architecture. It captures environmental and social value that markets leave unpriced, such as carbon reductions and biodiversity outcomes, as verified data and settles it in stable payment units, so that evidence of environmental integrity triggers payment without discretionary intervention.

Problem it addressesClean air, carbon reduction, and biodiversity create large positive externalities, yet legacy accounting cannot record them, and annual after-the-fact reporting leaves room for greenwashing.

The monetization function

V_monetize = Σ ( E_k × P_standard × M_k × e^(−σ·t_k) )

TermMeaning in the book
E_kPhysical outcome achieved: carbon reduced, area of biodiversity conserved, or protected-species population maintained
P_standardThe standard conversion price for that domain
M_kIntegrity coefficient of the underlying evidence, between 0 and 1
e^(−σ·t_k)Discount for the decay risk of data-collection equipment and the passage of time

The book introduces programmable eco-capital as the concept behind the function: environmental performance internalized into the three layers of programmable money, so that verified integrity in the data layer triggers settlement in the value layer.

Four settlement stages

1

Data harvesting

IoT sensors, satellite radar, and biometric tags collect outcome data off-chain.

2

Cryptographic hashing

The metadata is hashed and recorded so that it cannot be altered.

3

Consensus verification

Device signatures, independent AI verification, and regulator-qualified nodes must agree; distorted data is rejected.

4

Eco-settlement

Once integrity is confirmed, the payment token is sent to the supplier’s wallet.

Privacy and comparison

To bring large companies in without exposing process or cost data, the book proposes a zk-ESG proof: a zero-knowledge proof that a performance standard was met, verified on-chain without revealing the underlying records.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Exogenous Value Monetization Model?

The Exogenous Value Monetization Model (외생 가치 화폐화 모델), proposed by Jace Shim in Target Money (2026), is the fourth vector of his Four Vectors of Trust Architecture. It captures environmental and social value that markets leave unpriced, such as carbon reductions and biodiversity outcomes, as verified data and settles it in stable payment units, so that evidence of environmental integrity triggers payment without discretionary intervention.

What is programmable eco-capital?

Programmable eco-capital is the book’s term for environmental performance internalized into the three layers of programmable money (value, contract, and data and AI), so that verified environmental integrity in the data layer triggers settlement in the value layer.

How is exogenous value calculated in the model?

The book defines V_monetize as the sum over outcomes of E_k × P_standard × M_k × e^(−σ·t_k): the physical outcome, a standard conversion price, an evidence-integrity coefficient between 0 and 1, and a discount for equipment decay and elapsed time.

How does the model address greenwashing?

It replaces annual after-the-fact reporting with evidence that must pass consensus among device signatures, independent AI verification, and regulator-qualified nodes before settlement, and it rejects distorted data.

What are Jaguar, Koala, and Honeybee Credits?

They are the book’s three biodiversity credit scenarios: jaguar protection in the Amazon, koala habitat and eucalyptus forest recovery in Australia, and honeybee density for crop pollination, each paid when sensor and AI evidence meets the agreed threshold.

Who proposed the Exogenous Value Monetization Model?

Jace Shim (심준규) proposed it in Chapter 11 of his book Target Money (Raon Book, 2026; Korean edition 《타겟 머니》, ISBN 979-11-24852-03-3) as Vector 4 of the Four Vectors of Trust Architecture.
