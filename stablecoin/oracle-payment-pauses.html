When should an automated payment pause?

Definition · Scoped Payment Hold RuleThe scoped payment hold rule, proposed by Jace Shim (2026), limits a payment hold to the obligations whose evidence is materially uncertain, with wider suspension reserved for uncertainty that cannot be isolated. It compares the expected loss from an erroneous release with the cost of holding and reviewing a legitimate payment: hold when q × L exceeds H, subject to contractual requirements that override the comparison.

Key ideaA missing meter update should not ordinarily stop every payment.

The case for a scoped hold

An oracle supplies external information to a payment system. A signature authenticates a reporting source while leaving measurement accuracy open, and two reports can agree because both use the same meter or intermediary.

The relevant questionWhether the evidence is sufficiently reliable for the decision at hand, including whether its age, coverage, and provenance meet the agreement.

A rule for paying, holding, and resuming

qEstimated probability that an obligation is materially incorrect
LIncremental loss from paying it before review, net of realistic recovery
HIncremental cost of a hold, including delay to the recipient and review

Hold when q × L > H

Contractual requirements override the comparison. Evaluate L relative to post-review payment. When q is poorly identified, apply a documented range and escalate cases that straddle the decision. Separate supported amounts from disputed amounts wherever the contract permits.

Application and evaluation

Replay historical decisions using only evidence available at each cutoff. Compare unconditional release, a system-wide pause, and a scoped hold at equal payment volume.

Frequently asked questions

When should automated payments pause?

The scoped payment hold rule, proposed by Jace Shim (2026), limits a payment hold to the obligations whose evidence is materially uncertain, with wider suspension reserved for uncertainty that cannot be isolated. It compares the expected loss from an erroneous release with the cost of holding and reviewing a legitimate payment: hold when q × L exceeds H, subject to contractual requirements that override the comparison.

Should a missing oracle update stop every payment?

Not ordinarily. The rule limits the hold to obligations whose evidence is materially uncertain and reserves wider suspension for uncertainty that cannot be isolated, such as a shared reporting outage, where all obligations relying on that source are assessed.

What is the decision rule for holding a payment?

Let q be the estimated probability that an obligation is materially incorrect, L the incremental loss from paying it before review, net of realistic recovery, and H the incremental cost of a hold. Hold when q × L exceeds H, subject to contractual requirements that override the comparison. When q is poorly identified, apply a documented range and escalate cases that straddle the decision.

Does a signed oracle report prove the data is accurate?

No. A signature authenticates a reporting source while leaving measurement accuracy open, and two reports can agree because both use the same meter or intermediary. The question is whether the evidence’s age, coverage, and provenance meet the agreement.

How does a held payment resume?

A time limit triggers authorized review, and fresh evidence or a recorded adjudication supports release. If a transfer already cleared, the parties reconcile through the agreed adjustment or recovery process.

Who proposed the scoped payment hold rule?
