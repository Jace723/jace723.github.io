Research

  • ESG

    How buyer-perceived ESG activities relate to trust and collaboration between firms and to supply chain performance, alongside ESG strategy, sustainability reporting, and AA1000-based assurance practice. A verified carbon settlement model links climate finance to correction and duplicate-claim controls, and frameworks on AI and ESG disclosure are in preparation.

  • AI Marketing

    In Winner Takes the Answer (Korean edition: AI 승자독식), I proposed four models for competing in AI-generated answers: 3C-AI for AI-mediated competition, VRIO-D for discoverability, STQP for customer decision questions, and E-E-A-T-C for citability.

  • Stablecoin & Digital Money

    Building on Dollar on Chain and Target Money, I proposed eleven models: the Stablecoin Reserve–Distribution Feedback Model, a bargaining model of who controls stablecoin payment networks, and an evaluation model for when programmable vouchers improve public spending, the Value–Purpose–Evidence Model of programmable money, the Agent–Purpose Binding Model for paying for care, a milestone settlement model for cross-border freelance contracts, the tax of distrust, a method for counting the hidden costs of conditional payments, verified carbon settlement for paying for emissions reductions, portable reputation for small-supplier finance, deposit-free merchant entry for stablecoin payment networks, and a break-even rule for when AI agents should pay per API call.

  • Business Model Innovation

    I examine how firms create and deliver value as technology and customer behavior change, and how AI, platforms, and data reshape that value.
